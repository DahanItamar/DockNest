# DockNest 2.6.0

See all your servers on one map, and move a site to another server by dragging it.

## New

- **Map.** All your servers and the sites on each one in one picture, with zoom and Fit to screen. On phones it is a list.
- **Move a site.** Drag a site onto another server, press **Save changes**, then **Move**. DockNest backs it up, tests a copy on the new server and only then switches. Cancel any time before the switch; **Switch back** for 7 days. Administrators only, beta.

## Fixed

- Access: Status and Role are now separate columns, and Active shows its icon.
- Storage cards: the buttons fill the card's width.

## Before you update

- Finish or cancel any move before going back to an older version.
