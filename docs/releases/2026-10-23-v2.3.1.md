# DockNest 2.3.1

Released 2026-10-23. [Full changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Summary

Phone fixes for the 2.3.0 redesign, found on an iPhone 17 Pro, and a simpler Update Center.

## What's new

None.

## Improved

- The Update Center has two tabs, Overview and What's new. Past updates appear in a "Recent updates" section at the bottom of the Overview, closed by default, as short rows that fit any phone. Old links to the History tab open the Overview.
- The Automatic checks card has only its switch; the duplicate "Turn off" row is gone.

## Fixed

- On phones the header no longer overlaps the logo; the tagline is hidden and sign-out moves to Settings > End session.
- The phone menu shows one close button instead of two.
- Card buttons on phones no longer overlap the content above them.
- The Backups, Sites, Servers, Jobs and Audit lists no longer leave empty space on phones and use a compact one-row pager.

## Security

No security changes.

## Upgrade notes

From 2.3.0, use **Update now** in the Update Center, or `scripts/upgrade.sh --version v2.3.1`. No metadata migration.

## Known limits

Unchanged from [2.2.0](2026-10-22-v2.2.0.md).

## Validation

Browser suites with mocked data at 320 to 480 px (including 402 x 874), light and dark. Pending the private release validation on the release commit.
