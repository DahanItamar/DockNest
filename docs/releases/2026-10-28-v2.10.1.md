# DockNest 2.10.1

A fix for adding connections, and a cleaner Storage page and sidebar.

## Better

- **Add storage destination** now opens in its own window instead of a form at the bottom of the Storage page.
- The sidebar is simpler: the System health card is gone.

## Fixed

- In **New connection**, choosing a provider no longer leaves the window looking empty and unclickable. The provider list now opens right under the field.
