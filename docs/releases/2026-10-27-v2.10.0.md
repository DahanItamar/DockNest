# DockNest 2.10.0

Alerts can now reach you almost anywhere, and their logins have one home.

## New

- **Alert channels.** Send alerts to WhatsApp, Discord, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat, Mattermost, ntfy, Gotify, Pushover, Matrix or your own webhook URL, as well as Telegram and email.
- **Connections.** Administration, **Connections** keeps the logins your alerts use: see which alert nodes use each one, send a test, replace a secret or delete one that is no longer used.
- **Operators can manage alerts.** An administrator can let operators set up alerts too. Off until you turn it on.

## Better

- **Clearer alerts.** Every alert has a title, the site and server, a plain reason and a link that opens the job in DockNest.
- **Alert nodes on the Map** are a provider plus a connection. Press Delete or use the icon on a node's corner to remove it.

## Before you update

- Your Telegram and email alerts keep working with no action. Their logins now appear in **Connections**.
- Alerts ignore `HTTPS_PROXY` and other proxy settings: DockNest connects to each service directly.
- A server on your own network, such as a self-hosted ntfy, needs **Allow private network** in Connections settings.
